# Problem review (2026-11-18)

**Who reviewed:** the same AI assistant that wrote the problems, so this is **not an independent review**.
A review by a teacher or JEE student is still recommended; record their name here if one is done.

**What was checked for each of the 50 problems:**
1. Step 1 transcribes the statement faithfully (every number, operation and unit).
2. The final answer was re-derived by hand (in addition to the SymPy checker proving every step).
3. Difficulty, relative to JEE Main.

**Result:** 50/50 pass checks 1 and 2. On difficulty, 14 are below JEE level (single-formula or textbook
drills) and 36 are Class 11-12 board level on JEE topics. None reaches typical JEE Main difficulty
(multi-concept problems). The README therefore says "school-board level on JEE topics", not "JEE-style".
The 14 easy problems are listed in V2.md as upgrade candidates; they were not replaced in v1 because the
physics ones supply most `unit_mixup` items (42, against the required 40).
